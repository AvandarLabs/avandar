# Choosing a video story

Use one audience, one problem, and an evidence-backed product sequence per film.
Keep company briefs and priorities under `.videos/<project>/`. The tracked
storyboards are examples using clearly labeled fictional Harborfield data.

The sample flows cover import, Explorer, dashboards, case records, and maps.
Read their storyboard source notes and adapter limitations before reusing them.
Generated fixture state does not establish that a live backend operation works.
Preserve blank values and dataset grain. Product footage requires its own browser
evidence. The migration's internal prioritization notes are retained locally in
`.videos/migration-archive/docs/`.
