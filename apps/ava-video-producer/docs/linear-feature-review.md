# Reviewing feature status for a video

Keep Linear exports, backlog audits, workspace identifiers, and internal planning
notes in the project's ignored `.videos/<project>/` directory. They are not sample
assets and do not belong in the showcase. The migration's earlier audit is kept
locally in `.videos/migration-archive/docs/`.

For each claim, distinguish a completed ticket from current source and observed
browser behavior. A completed issue alone is not proof that a product flow works.
Read product code from this worktree and retain approved evidence beside the
private storyboard. Do not send messages or update issues as part of a review.
