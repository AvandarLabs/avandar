The original 100 stable IDs, ordering and packet assignments remain intact. This expansion has no new numerical quota. A concept earns a new item only when its visible explanatory action differs from the existing 100, the 20 runtime entries and the other source proposals. Repeated principles become coverage links or enrich existing metadata, rather than receiving renamed duplicates. The coordinator reviewed every candidate and retained explicit neighbor distinctions. Coverage links are conceptual storyboard recommendations; they do not certify that a whole source workflow fits one existing prop contract. Compose bounded scenes where needed, and leave explicitly mentioned extensions as future work.

`coverage` rows describe source concepts, not unique animations. Several rows can map to one accepted item. A `new` row identifies an item introduced in this expansion, even when another source group owns its candidate. `enrich` adds use cases, vocabulary, constraints or direction to a retained ID. `covered` already has a suitable action. `unsuitable` is too broad, confidential, unverified, biographical or unsupported to become a stock mechanism.

## Source status and retrieval boundary

- Business strategy and values motivate concepts; they do not verify shipped capabilities, measured effects or policies currently in force.
- Architecture documents mix current descriptions, designs, historical plans and implementation gaps. A source hash identifies the inspected snapshot. Status is qualified in the inventory and coverage reasons, not converted into product claims.
- Schema-only remote planning sends a question and structural metadata while executing returned queries locally. User-authored questions can contain sensitive terms. Other optional sample/excerpt paths have distinct disclosure behavior. Neither this catalog nor the inspected source supports a universal assertion that every AI workflow sends no data.
- Source paths, document names, hashes, locators, proposal history and coordinator decisions stay in planning provenance. Runtime metadata and future embedding text use generic semantic fields only, through an explicit allowlist.
- The source PDFs remain authoritative in the vault. Repository docs remain authoritative for their described snapshots. These generic animation contracts are illustrative designs, not a replacement architecture specification.
