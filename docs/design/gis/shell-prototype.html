Color ramps and legends

Every value below was generated in OKLCH and checked with the dataviz validator, not picked by eye. Sequential ramps are monotone in lightness with an adjacent step separation of at least 0.064, so class order survives greyscale photocopying. Use the greyscale button to check it.

Sequential, for graduated color

Blue and green are deliberately absent as defaults. Blue is the app's accent and the basemap's water; green is the basemap's landcover. A thematic fill in either competes with the map underneath it.

What greyscale breaks, and what carries it instead

Press the greyscale button above and two things collapse, both of them by nature rather than by a bad choice of hex.

A diverging ramp cannot survive greyscale by tone. Both arms are light at the midpoint and dark at the poles, so violet-4 and ochre-4 print as the same grey. In the export, the two arms take opposite hatch angles instead: 45° below the baseline, 135° above. The angle carries the sign; the tone carries the magnitude.

A categorical map cannot survive greyscale by fill. Distinct hues at similar lightness are one grey. Categorical layers therefore carry direct labels on the map, which is also the relief the contrast check requires: four of the eight categorical slots sit below 3:1 on a light basemap.

Absence values

Both are true neutrals at OKLCH chroma 0, well under the sequential ramps' chroma floor of 0.056, so neither can read as a low class. Both carry a texture, because no grey can be separated from a mid class by tone alone once the page is photocopied. Suppressed is darker and hatched: there is something there, and it is being withheld.
